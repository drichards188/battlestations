# How to play

Back to the [README](../README.md).

## Starting a battle

A cinematic camera tours the task group behind the title screen while the fleet fights off a practice raid. Pick a difficulty (Recruit, Gunner or Veteran), **Day** or **Night**, and click **Man your station**.

Switching Day and Night on the title screen changes the sky and the practice raids behind it at once; during a battle it applies to the next one.

## General Quarters

The battle opens below decks with the general alarm and the 1MC call to general quarters.

- **The run.** You turn out of your rack in a second-deck berthing compartment and run with your shipmates "up and forward to starboard": through the watertight door, up the inclined ladders and hatches to the main deck and the 01 level, out of the weather door, up the ladder to the sponson and into Mount 47's tub.
- **The crew.** The men ahead keep their distance on the ladders and peel off aft to their own stations once you are out on deck.
- **The gun.** The camera settles onto the gun, and the raid waits until you are on it.
- **Skipping it.** Mouse-look works on the way; `Space` skips to the gun, and the settings panel can turn the opening off.

## On the gun

You start on Mount 47, the starboard quad 40mm Bofors amidships on the 02 level. Raids are called out by radar with their bearing, range and altitude, and show up as red markers on the compass.

- **Aiming.** Train and elevate with the mouse: you are working the pointer's and trainer's handwheels. The mount's electro-hydraulic power drives follow at their real speed (26 degrees a second in train, 24 in elevation for the Mk 2 quad).
- **A heavy mount.** The drives accelerate and brake no harder than their gear allows, and a moment late, so you can't whip the mount round. A small circle shows where you are commanding it to point, and it can't run more than about 30 degrees ahead of the guns.
- **Firing.** Hold the left button. The quad fires at its real rate, each gun cycling about every half second; the guns of each twin slide alternate through their delay gear, so one gun of each pair fires at the same moment.
- **Leading.** The rounds take seconds to reach a target, so lead it. On Recruit and Gunner the **lead computing sight** puts a diamond where you need to aim at the aircraft nearest your bore line.
- **Heat.** Barrels heat up under long bursts and lock out until they cool.
- **Tracers.** Every 40mm round carries a tracer, one round in five in a 20mm drum (its tracer burns out after about 3.75 s while the round flies on), and the 5" fire none.
- **Views.** `X` switches between the centre view and the pointer's seat behind his ring sight.
- **Firing cutout.** The guns stop firing rather than shoot through your own superstructure.

## The Bofors feed

Each gun's automatic loader is a pair of curved guides above the rammer tray holding seven rounds, noses forward, fanning toward the muzzle, with an eighth on the tray.

- **Each shot.** The feed pawls force the column down one round, the star wheels turn a quarter turn and the lowest round drops onto the tray. The empty case goes out of the breech into the deflector and down through the carriage to the deck.
- **Clips.** Loaders drop 4-round clips into the feed lips. Each clip rides down with its rounds until it is cammed off two rounds from the bottom and thrown out through the side of the slide.
- **The loader catch.** With two rounds left the catch stops the gun, and it fires again by itself the moment a clip goes in.
- **Human loaders.** They tire, a crooked clip has to be rocked into the lips, and now and then one jams and has to be cleared with the tools while that gun runs dry.
- **The supply.** Second loaders work out of the ready racks, passers restock the racks from the lockers and the hoist refills the lockers. Hold the trigger long enough and you outrun them.
- **The HUD** shows each gun's tray round, its seven in the guides and the loader's next clip, plus the ready supply.

## Every mount aboard

Every anti-aircraft gun aboard Alabama can be manned: her ten twin 5"/38 mounts, twelve quad 40mm Bofors and thirty-three single 20mm Oerlikons. Whatever you aren't on is crewed by the AI, like every gun in the fleet.

- **The ship's plan.** Hold `Ctrl` to see Alabama from above with every mount marked, pointing the way it is trained. Hover one to see its arc of fire, use the tabs to pick out the 5", 40mm or 20mm, and click a mount to take it over. The battle doesn't stop while the plan is up; let go of `Ctrl` to go back to your gun.
- **The 5".** The mounts are enclosed and slow to train (25 degrees a second, 15 in elevation). Each gun is hand-loaded from the hoists and fires as its breech closes, about 15 to 20 rounds a minute a gun, with VT-fuzed shells that burst on proximity. `X` moves you from the mount captain's hatch to the pointer's telescope window.
- **The 20mm.** The Oerlikons are fast and light, with 60-round drums (`R` to change drums).
- **Numbers.** The 5" mounts are numbered 1 to 10, the 40mm quads 41 to 52 and the 20mm by our own numbers.

## Day

Six raids escalate through the afternoon into golden hour: dive bombers, a torpedo attack, a coordinated strike from two sides, the special attack unit (kamikazes), land-based Bettys, then everything at once.

- **Dive bombers** push over from about 2 km and release around 450 m.
- **Torpedo planes** drop to wave-top height on a ship's beam, drop about a kilometre out, then break away or roar across the formation. Torpedo tracks show as white wakes, and ships turn to comb them.
- **Zeros** make strafing runs and pull up over the deck.
- **Kamikazes** weave in and keep coming even when they are on fire.
- **Damage.** Bombs and kamikazes start fires; damage control parties restore some hull integrity between raids.
- **Winning.** Lose your own ship or the carrier and the battle is over. Survive all six for the after-action report, then keep fighting in an endless mode if you like.

## Night

Six night raids follow the pattern of the Japanese attacks on the fast carriers in 1943 and 1944.

- **The snooper** shadows the force at dusk and drops a line of float lights on the water to mark its track.
- **Flare planes** then hang parachute flares beyond the ships, so the torpedo planes see them in silhouette.
- **The torpedo planes** (Bettys, with Kates standing in for the newer Jills) come in low, most from one side and a few from another, with a few bombers gliding in by flare light. There is no fighter escort and nobody strafes.
- **The sky.** The first attack comes out of the darkening east while the afterglow still silhouettes the ships to the west; the rest come after dark under a waxing crescent moon setting in the west.
- **Darkened ships.** Radar calls the raids as bogeys (nobody can see what type they are).
- **Gunnery.** The 5" fire by radar with VT fuzes as by day, but the 40mm and 20mm are aimed by eye. They hold fire at aircraft beyond about 1.2 km unless a flare lights them up or they are already burning. The attackers' aim is worse too.
- **Finding the planes.** Look for their exhaust flames, flare light, and the tracers going up at them. Parachute flares light the ships, the sea and the cloud bases, and burn for a couple of minutes while they drift down; the sea's wakes glow faintly.
- **Your eyes adapt.** The exposure follows the scene, and the darks lose their colour toward blue as night vision does.
- **Winning.** Survive all six for the after-action report.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Mouse | train and elevate the gun |
| Left button | fire |
| Right button | zoom the sight |
| hold `Ctrl` | the ship's plan: click a mount to man it |
| `1`-`9`, `Q` / `E` | change mount |
| `R` | change drums (20mm) |
| `X` | centre view or the pointer's sight |
| `Space` | skip the General Quarters run to the gun |
| `C` | watch the battle from the cinematic camera (and back) |
| `P` | photo mode (freezes time, free camera, depth of field) |
| `W` `A` `S` `D`, `Space` / `Q`, `Shift` | photo mode: fly the camera, up / down, faster |
| `K` | capture a PNG screenshot (photo mode) |
| `V` | cycle weather: Clear, Cloudy, Rain, Storm |
| `M` | mute |
| `H` | hide all UI |
| `F3` | FPS, draw calls, triangles, aircraft, shells, particles |
| `Esc` | release the mouse (pauses the battle, settings panel) |

`Esc` also opens the settings panel: quality presets (Low to Ultra), dynamic resolution, mouse sensitivity, field of view, color grade, the lead computing sight (Auto, On or Off), the General Quarters opening and more.

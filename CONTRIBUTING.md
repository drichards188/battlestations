# Contributing to Battle Stations

Contributions are welcome, here in this repository.

Battle Stations is source-available, not open source. Its [license](LICENSE) (PolyForm Strict 1.0.0) lets you play and study it for non-commercial purposes. It does not let you change it, publish your own version of it, or use it commercially.

## Permission to contribute

As an exception to the license, you may fork this repository on GitHub and change your fork, only to propose those changes back to this repository as pull requests. This permission does not cover publishing, distributing or using a changed version for anything else.

## Your contribution

By opening a pull request, you agree that:

- the contribution is your own work, or you have the right to submit it;
- you grant David Richards a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable license to use, change, distribute and sublicense it under any terms, including commercially and under other licenses;
- you keep your own copyright in it.

## How

- Open an issue first for anything large, so we can agree on the approach.
- [Development](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md) covers sharing the build, headless testing and the source layout.
- Say in the pull request how you checked the change, in the browser or headless.
